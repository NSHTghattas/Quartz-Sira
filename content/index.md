### Warning: This setting is still a Work in Progress

Welcome to the official Sira wiki!

Sira is a worldbuilding project that I have been working on for nearly a year and a half now, since the beginning of summer 2024. It is a fantasy world set in the equivalent of the Middle Ages, taking heavy inspiration from Brandon Sanderson's works, Games Workshop's Warhammer 40,000, Paradox Interactive's World of Darkness, Pirateaba's The Wandering Inn, and more.

I have many narrative projects in the works for this setting as I expand on it, not least among them *[[The Steel Legionnaires]]* and *The Taskmaster of Ninovacia*, the current rough drafts of which are available for reading.

All basic, easy to digest information can be found in the [[Sira Primer]] if you want a quick overview of the world and it's history. For more information, you can check out the following categories:

**Geography**: The geography of the five continents, environmental and otherwise.

**Geopolitics:** An in depth look into each of the four empires and their vassal nations.

**History**: The history of the world dating all the way back to roughly 1500 years ago

**Media**: Where I store wiki articles of my current works, as well as the rough drafts I feel is good enough to put there.

**People**: A look into every important person in the setting, as well as various people found in my stories.

**Races**: An overview of each race in Sira, only really useful if you want to run a game in this setting.
