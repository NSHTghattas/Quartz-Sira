title: Talent
draft: true
---
Talent is a way of magic that has been present in Sira for a long time. The exact date is not known, but most scholars agree that it could not be before about 300 P.S. Talent is an inherently genetic power that few people are born with, with a more common--if weaker--variant.

Talent originated in the Brass Empire, thought to be bestowed on the Drakken and Lizardken alone by Oreden'no, the Dragon.
