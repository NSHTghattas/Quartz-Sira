As of today, Sira has five continents, four of which are habitable. In order of landmass descending, the continents are called The Steel Continent, The Brass Continent, The Bronze Continent, The Golden Continent and The Sapphire Isles.

# The Steel Continent
Home to [[The Steel Kingdom]], it is the largest continent that formed as a result of the Sundering. It has one of the most varied climates of the world, having an icy northern tundra, slowly turning into lush green plains the further south one goes, until at the southern most part of the continent where moving forests and mountains dominate near the coast.

# The Brass Continent
Home to [[The Brass Empire]], it is the second largest continent.
