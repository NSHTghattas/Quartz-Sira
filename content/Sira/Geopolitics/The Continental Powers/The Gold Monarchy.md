The Gold Monarchy is a purely Monarchic nation located at the Gold Continent, the smallest of all continents beside the Sapphire Isles. It is ruled by the Golden Monarch. It is predominantly made up of Dwarves, though it also boasts the highest population of all the other races from differing nations, being the most friendly to outsiders.
# The Capital
The Shining Capital of Beauty (colloquially referred to as "The Capital"), is the capital city of the Gold Monarchy.
